# Minimum Initial Energy to Finish Tasks

- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-initial-energy-to-finish-tasks/

## Problem

Given an array `tasks` where `tasks[i] = [actuali, minimumi]`, where `actuali` is the actual energy spent to finish the task and `minimumi` is the minimum energy required to begin it. Return the minimum initial energy needed to finish all tasks in any order.
